Noise Blueprint
Extracts multi-scale high-frequency noise residuals (median diff, Gaussian high-pass, Laplacian). Real cameras produce uniform physical grain; fakes show smooth/uneven synthetic noise.
✓ Real: Natural sensor grain ▲ Fake: Smooth / irregular texture
🎨
Color Balance & HSV
Evaluates RGB channel balance, HSV saturation distribution and LAB color residual shifts. Deepfake blending leaves unnatural color boundary transitions.
✓ Real: Balanced HSV spectrum ▲ Fake: Color boundary distortion
🔬
Grayscale Luminance
Analyses luminance consistency and multi-scale Laplacian edge responses. AI generation introduces unnatural edge gradients around face contours.
✓ Real: Uniform lighting ▲ Fake: Irregular edges
⏱️
Temporal Forensics
(Video only)
Measures inter-frame optical flow shifts, noise variance jitter and flickering across extracted frames. Deepfake videos show high temporal incoherence.
✓ Real: Smooth motion ▲ Fake: High flicker / jitter
